Refund requests, settled by your policy.

Claude reads your refund policy and each customer message. PayPal tells the desk what was paid and refunded. Code decides the amount.

Try the demo Open the seller desk

  1. Policy becomes rules

    Claude turns your plain-English policy into rules. Every rule quotes the sentence it came from, and you approve them.

  2. The request is read, not obeyed

    Claude classifies the message and flags text that tries to steer the refund.

  3. PayPal supplies the facts

    Captured amount, earlier refunds and open disputes come from the PayPal APIs, checked again right before any refund.

  4. Small, clean cases settle themselves

    Under your limit with every check passing, the refund runs through PayPal and the reply goes out. Everything else waits for one click.

PayPal in this build

  • JavaScript SDK buttons and Orders v2 (create, capture, details)
  • Payments v2 refunds with idempotency keys
  • Disputes API check before money moves
  • Webhooks with signature verification

Claude in this build

  • Policy compiler with word-for-word quotes
  • Request reading with manipulation flags
  • Replies in the customer's language, amount-checked
  • Structured outputs; no tool can move money

Five-minute tour

  1. In the seller desk, compile the sample policy and approve the rules.
  2. Buy Quiet Hours Pro in the demo store with a PayPal sandbox buyer account.
  3. On the receipt, open Ask for a refund and send one of the sample messages.
  4. Watch the case in the seller desk: the PayPal facts, the rule it matched, every check, and the refund ID.
  5. Buy the Team plan and ask again: above the auto limit, the desk waits for your click.
